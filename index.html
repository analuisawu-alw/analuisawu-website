Visa Benefits Portal
Product

Visa Benefits Portal

An API-first, Salesforce-backed SaaS platform that gives cardholders one place to discover and use 30+ benefits: travel insurance certificates, claims, concierge and real-time request tracking. Banks embed it in their own digital channels.

The problem

When I started, there was no precedent and no playbook. Benefits across 40 markets ran on fragmented, manual operations, and a lot of people worked very hard to hold them together. My job was to see the system underneath and make it simple enough to scale.

My role

Owned requirements, roadmap and end-to-end delivery. Grew the delivery team to 30 and ran rollout across 40 markets and 500 banks.
